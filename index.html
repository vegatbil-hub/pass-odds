From where you stand: load your live account

Run the small snapshot script on your own computer or in Colab. It reads your account through the MyFundedPerps API with your key from an environment variable, and prints numbers only: equity, floors, room left, distance to target. Paste them here. Your key never touches this page.

How it works

    
Each simulated trade wins reward × risk with your win rate, or loses risk. Up to your trades per day, stopping early after your loss limit. The account fails the moment equity touches the static floor, or drops below start-of-day equity minus the daily allowance. It passes the moment equity touches the target.

    
A passed account starts funded at the same balance and floors, and is followed for 60 trading days: at the end of each day, if profit is at least your payout goal, it is withdrawn. On Elite the floor never moves; on Select and Signature it locks at the start balance after the first payout, so the simulated trader keeps half a max loss as a cushion. Challenge results are counted within your patience; whatever is unresolved is "still running".

    
Same inputs, same numbers: the random generator is seeded.

  






  Assumptions and sources
  
    
    
  • Rules from the MyFundedPerps docs, read on 24 November 2026: Challenges and rules, Elite accounts, Payouts. Elite list prices from the Elite guide; sales change them, so type what you pay.
    • 
    
  • Every trade risks the same amount and ends at its stop or its target. Real trading has slippage, fees, partial exits and correlated days: real odds are usually a little worse.
    • 
    
  • Your win rate and reward to risk are the inputs that matter most, and the hardest to know. Use at least 50 of your own logged trades, not your best week.
    • 
    
  • "Back per $1" counts the cash you would receive in your first 60 funded days, after the split, divided by the price. It ignores later payouts, and the cost of your time.
